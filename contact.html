Before you write

Including the following helps us find the problem faster:

  • Your device model and OS version (for example, iPhone 13 / iOS 26.5)
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of the in-game settings screen
  • What you were doing when the problem happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one

Purchases and refunds

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and refunds follow the policy of the store you bought from. Please submit refund requests to the store directly.

  • Apple App Store — reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play — Play Store › Menu › Payments & subscriptions › Order history

If you paid but the content did not unlock, please email us. Include your order number and we will look into it.

Privacy requests

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, email us at the address above. See the Privacy Policy for details.

결제와 환불

결제는 구글 플레이 또는 Apple App Store가 처리하며, 환불은 구매하신 스토어의 정책을 따릅니다. 환불 요청은 해당 스토어로 직접 접수해 주시기 바랍니다.

  • Apple App Store — reportaproblem.apple.com
  • 구글 플레이 — Play 스토어 › 메뉴 › 결제 및 정기 결제 › 주문 내역

결제는 됐는데 콘텐츠가 열리지 않는 경우에는 주문번호와 함께 이메일로 알려주세요. 확인해 드리겠습니다.
