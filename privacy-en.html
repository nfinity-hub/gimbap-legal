Privacy Policy

Gimbap
Effective date: 17 August 2026

Nfinity (the "Developer") respects your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This policy applies to the mobile game Gimbap (the "Service").

1. Information We Process

The Service does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number. We process the following:

CategoryDataHow it is collected
Anonymous identifier A randomly generated identifier (GUID) created the first time you open the app. It cannot be used to identify you personally. Generated automatically on first launch
Device information Device model, operating system version, memory size, screen resolution, language setting, app version Collected automatically when the app runs
Gameplay records Game progress, play time, launch and exit records, in-game choices and their outcomes, screen navigation, errors and crash reports Collected automatically during play
Information you provide Content you write and send using the feedback feature Submitted by you
Purchase records Product purchased, time of purchase, order number, verification result. Payment details are not included. Collected automatically when you buy paid content
Notification token A per-device identifier used to deliver notifications. It cannot be used to identify you personally. Issued if you allow notifications. Not used if you turn them off in settings

We do not collect the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or date of birth
  • Advertising identifiers (ADID / IDFA) or persistent device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, and the like)
  • Location data, contacts, photos, or call logs
  • Payment details such as credit card numbers. Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and the Developer never receives your payment information.

2. Why We Process It

  • To provide and maintain the game service
  • To identify and fix errors and crashes
  • To improve game difficulty and balance
  • To read and act on feedback you send
  • To verify purchases, confirm your entitlements, and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • To send announcements and game news, only if you have allowed notifications

We do not use this information for any other purpose, and we never use it for advertising or marketing.

3. Legal Basis (for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom)

  • Performance of a contract — processing needed to provide the game and to deliver content you have purchased.
  • Legitimate interests — diagnosing errors and improving game balance. We limit this to anonymous and technical data.
  • Consent — analytics collection, which you review on the consent screen shown the first time you open the app. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting the app or by contacting us.

4. How Long We Keep It

  • Data is deleted without delay one year after collection.
  • Data is deleted without delay if you ask us to delete it.
  • Where law requires us to retain records, we keep them for the required period.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

The Developer does not sell or share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

You may object to international transfer. Please note that if you do, you may not be able to use parts of the Service. Contact us at the address below to object.

7. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Request access to the information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion
  • Request that we stop processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. We may need your anonymous identifier to locate your data. You can also use the account deletion feature inside the app, and deleting the app stops any further collection.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority.

8. Deletion

Information is deleted without delay once the retention period ends or the purpose of processing is fulfilled. Electronically stored data is permanently erased in a way that cannot be recovered.

9. Security

  • We do not collect directly identifying information; we use only an anonymous identifier.
  • Access to collected data is limited to the smallest number of people necessary.
  • All data in transit is protected with encrypted connections (HTTPS).

10. Automatic Collection and How to Refuse It

The Service automatically collects gameplay records. You can review what is collected on the consent screen shown the first time you open the app, and collection stops when you delete the app.

11. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected such information, we delete it without delay.

For users in the Republic of Korea, the Personal Information Protection Act requires consent from a legal guardian for children under 14. If we learn that a child under 14 has provided information without such consent, we delete it without delay.

If you believe your child's information has been collected, please contact us at the address below.

14. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will post the change in the app or on this page. Where the change is significant, we will ask for your consent again the next time you open the app.
